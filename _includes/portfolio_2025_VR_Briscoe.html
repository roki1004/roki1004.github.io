VR Briecoe (2024 - 2025)
- VR Meta Quest - Five - 13 Months - Unity
I participated in the development of an application that delivers a reflux experiment simulation to students. Building on the ObjectiveSystem previously developed for the Micropipette project, I enhanced it with expanded functionality and improved usability.
I was deeply involved in the creation of various shaders, including dummy hologram shaders for learning progression, highlight effects, and rendering order management across the entire application. Given the larger scale of this project, I placed even greater emphasis on optimization.
In addition to standard techniques like object pooling and caching, I utilized tools such as the Unity Profiler, Frame Debugger, and Meta VSS Debugger to identify precise optimization points and implement targeted improvements.
